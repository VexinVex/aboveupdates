What this update adds

The update is built around four connected areas: catching animals, turning them into ongoing producers, expanding island automation, and making everyday management easier.

🐾

Animal collecting

Catch chickens, sheep, cows and horses in the Ruins with a Lasso. Animals can be named and managed individually.

🥚

Passive production

Chickens produce eggs, cows produce milk, and sheep grow wool. Fed animals can keep producing while you're away.

⚙️

More automation

New machines harvest berries, package crops, collect dropped items and support much larger vending stockpiles.

🧭

Island management

New interface layouts, an island Limits tab, favorites for published islands, and easier chest collection.

🌸

Wild Bloom weather

Wild animals spawn twice as often, more can roam at once, and crops yield 5% more per harvest.

🛠️

Fixes and tuning

Performance improvements, conveyor behavior fixes, a Morten fix, and a temporary Arcane Hopper/shop warning.

New gameplay system

Animals & how to get them

All four animal types are found roaming around the Ruins. Getting close can make them run, so approach carefully.

How catching works: Visit the Animal Tamer in the Ruins to get a Lasso. Aim at a wild animal, throw the Lasso, then reel it in. The announcement does not specify catch odds, range, or whether different animals require different techniques.
🐔

Chickens

Hatch eggs in an Incubator to discover 16 breeds. Keep hens in a Hen House and supplied by a Chicken Feeder to collect eggs.

16 breeds • Up to 6 incubators
🐄

Cows

Keep cows near a Hay Feeder to collect milk. The developer mentions special milks, but doesn't list their effects.

Milk • Special milk types
🐑

Sheep

Sheep graze on grass to grow wool. Wool can be dyed in 21 colors and used to make wool blocks or carpet tiles.

Wool • 21 colors
🐎

Horses

Place a horse in your Mount slot, then press V to ride on PC or hold D-pad Up on console. Coat skins cost coins or Robux.

Rideable • Coat skins
🏷️

Name & manage animals

Every animal can be named. Open its Manage window to see what it needs and when its next egg, milk or wool will be ready.

🤠

Animal Tamer

The Animal Tamer lives in the Ruins. Visit them for a Lasso and animal-related information.

Animal care & timing

Production and offline progress

The key requirement is care: animals need to be fed or have access to the appropriate food source for production.

🥚

Egg production

Hens need a Hen House and Chicken Feeder. Collect their eggs from the Hen House. Fed hens continue laying while you're away.

🥛

Milk production

Keep cows near a Hay Feeder. Watch for special milk varieties; exact effects and production rates weren't included in the announcement.

🧶

Wool growth

Sheep graze grass to grow wool. The announcement doesn't give an exact wool growth timer.

Offline production rule: Animals produce at 50% while you're offline, for up to 8 hours, as long as they're fed before you leave.

What the 8-hour cap means

Only the first 8 hours offline count for this production rule. At half speed, that is equivalent to 4 hours of normal-rate production.

Before logging off

  1. Check each animal's needs in its Manage window.
  2. Make sure it has the required food available.
  3. Check whether production is nearly ready to collect.
New equipment

Machines & buildable items

New tools for harvesting, packaging, collecting, selling, displaying gear and building with wool.

🍒

Berry Harvester

Automates berry harvesting. The announcement calls out cooking recipes as a reason it can be useful.

🧺

Basket Machine

Packs tomatoes, onions, carrots or berries into baskets. Each basket sells for more than the crops inside. Also makes haybales from 100 wheat.

🌀

Arcane Hopper

Anything that falls into it goes straight into your backpack. Note the temporary performance issue when using it alongside vending machines.

🏪

Bulk Vending Machine

Sells up to 100,000 of one item, including unique machine, weapon and tool stacks.

🛡️

Armor Stand

Displays a full set of armor so you can show off an equipment setup.

🧶

Wool blocks & carpets

Use wool in all 21 colors to create wool blocks or carpet tiles.

Final details

Weather, fixes & developer notes

The remaining changes and clarifications from the announcement and its follow-up messages.

🌸

Wild Bloom weather

  • Wild animals appear in the Ruins right away.
  • Animals spawn twice as often.
  • More animals can roam at once.
  • Crops yield 5% more per harvest.
🔧

Bug fixes & performance

  • Items caught against conveyor belt walls now move back into the lane.
  • Fixed a way to dodge Morten's slam by stepping out of his grab.
  • Performance improved, especially on large islands.
Temporary issue — Arcane Hopper + Vending Machines: The Hopper is refreshing the vending UI too frequently, causing a performance issue while managing shops. The developer says a fix is coming. Until then, avoid running the Hopper while managing vending machines.
🌼

Flower pickup change

Flowers you've placed must now be broken with a pickaxe. Wild flowers and flower offspring can still be collected with F. This prevents accidental pickup of placed flowers and makes it easier to collect offspring.
